APPENDIX / TRANSPARENCY

Methods & limitations

A chronological boundary, not an explanation

This report examines performance on either side of November 18, 2022. It does not attribute the change to the arrest, an individual coach, or any single organizational decision.

Comparison windows

Equal calendar time: December 18, 2018–November 17, 2022 versus November 18, 2022–October 19, 2026. Equal games: the most recent N games before the event versus the first N games afterward, with N determined by the smaller sample.

Data provenance

Primary results: user-provided 2010-2026_scores.csv. The previously pending October 18, 2026 Indianapolis result was verified as TEN 26, IND 20 from the NFL game center ↗. No other scores were overwritten.

The nine uploaded seasonal play files cover all 125 selected results except Week 6 of 2026. All score-based metrics include that result; play-tag analyses do not.

Definitions and additional source limitations +

One-score game: a final margin of eight points or fewer. AFC South games: opponents HOU, IND, or JAX. Streak: consecutive results within the selected sample, not necessarily uninterrupted across excluded games in the equal-games comparison. Wins per 17: wins (plus half of any ties) divided by games played, multiplied by 17.

All primary comparisons use final regular-season scores. Postseason records and division titles are separate season-level outcomes and not added to regular-season win rates. The 2020 and 2021 AFC South titles are maintained as documented season metadata; this site does not attempt to reproduce NFL standings tiebreakers.

2026 Week 6 is absent from the supplied play-by-play archive. The exploratory play counts come from labeled outcomes and are not official NFL totals. Individual game score sources are shown in the game inspection panel where a supplemental source was required.
